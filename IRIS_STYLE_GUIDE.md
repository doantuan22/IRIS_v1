# IRIS UI Style Guide

## Tinh cach thi giac

IRIS la giao dien trang-xanh sang, nhe va de doc cho phu huynh. Uu tien khoang
trang rong, the trang bo tron, vien xanh rat nhat va bong do rat nhe.

## Thuong hieu va minh hoa

- Wordmark `IRIS` duoc ve bang Flutter: Fredoka dam, gradient xanh va mot ngoi sao bon canh.
- Khong su dung mascot, con vat, khuon mat dong vat hay asset giau y nghia mascot.
- Avatar ho so la minh hoa tre em flat-light, ba bien the nam/nu/trung tinh, nen trong suot.
- Icon cua bon chuc nang Home la asset minh hoa 3D-lite: bang kiem, camera,
  dong ho va bat tay/co trai tim. Khong thay cac icon nay bang Material/Cupertino.

## Mau sac

- Navy: `#102A6B`; primary blue: `#1769E8`; gradient blue: `#4B9BFF` -> `#1552CF`.
- Canvas: `#F8FBFF`; surface: trang; blue-soft: `#DDEBFF`; border: `#D9E7F8`.
- Nen trang tri dung blob xanh rat nhat, cham tron, dot-grid va sparkle vien xanh nhat.
- Trang thai AI: xanh la khi ca NVIDIA va Groq thanh cong, do khi co loi, xanh duong khi dang kiem tra.

## Component

- Display/wordmark dung Fredoka; noi dung dung Nunito.
- Card va profile dung nen trang, bo goc 18-20, border xanh nhat va shadow mem.
- Moi card chuc nang dung o vuong pastel ben trai va nut mui ten tron ben phai.
- Navigation bar nen trang; muc duoc chon co pill xanh nhat.
- Splash hien wordmark, spinner va trang thai AI song song voi bootstrap; AI khong duoc chan viec vao app.
